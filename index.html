About

arcverex builds reinforcement-learning environments that teach language models how to do real hardware engineering. We partner with frontier labs to train agents on production-grade RTL work.

Hardware is exactly the kind of open-ended, tool-rich domain that RL is built for. Our environments reward real engineering outcomes — not gaming the harness.

Each environment is a graded, sandboxed task drawn from real designs: read the spec, write the RTL, build the stimulus, close the loop. They teach methodology, not reward hacks.

You may already be using models trained on our environments. License our catalog, or commission environments built to your spec.
